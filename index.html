The tool reads your real resolution and frame rate on upload and pre-sets Enhance and Frame Interpolation accordingly. A greyed-out toggle just means your footage already exceeds that fix's useful range.

Enhance turns on automatically for footage at 1080p or below, since that's where it makes the most visible difference. It stays off above 1080p and is disabled at 4K, where there's no missing detail left to rebuild. It's most useful on soft phone or downloaded clips; it won't help pixelated screenshots, since no tool can invent detail that was never captured.
